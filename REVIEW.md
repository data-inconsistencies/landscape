# Review

Three passes. Findings inform the reviewer; a person approves.

1. **Facts**: name, link, repository and layer match the product's own site. The logo belongs to the product.
2. **Safety**: no scripts in SVG files, no secrets, nothing that force-pushes or deploys without a person.
3. **Spec**: the change follows `intent/spec.md`, and `intent/plan.md` is in step.

**Important**: a wrong fact, wrong logo, dead link, broken build or unsafe deploy. Everything else is a nit; list at
most five. Skip `build/` and the contents of vendor logos beyond pass 2.
